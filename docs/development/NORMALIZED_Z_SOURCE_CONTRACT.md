# Normalized Z Source Contract

`EXISTING_NORMALIZED_Z` means the source Z coordinate passed a bounded scientific validation as height above terrain. A filename such as `_Norm` or a plausible header range is only a candidate signal and cannot establish this mode.

Validation requires finite sampled values, non-constant vertical structure, plausible low/high quantiles, bounded negative fraction, near-ground support, and a class-2 ground median near zero when class 2 is present. Failed or unavailable evidence falls through to the established class-2 Delaunay or SMRF-plus-Delaunay preparation path.

When accepted, PDAL copies Z into a new explicit `HeightAboveGround` dimension in the local prepared artifact. Original Z is preserved. Downstream products consume the explicit dimension; they do not silently reinterpret Z.

For the Olaa source, the observed header range of approximately -7.078 to 23.643 metres makes normalized Z eligible for inspection, while the absence of an explicit `HeightAboveGround` dimension means preparation is still required. Live source access is required to complete the sample-based conclusion.
